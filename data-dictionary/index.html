JSON record structure

The nested evidence projection.

credential

Stable IDs, vendor and credential names, route semantics, official URL, and known exam codes.

currentness

State, current exam identity or version posture, first-party source, and checked date.

eligibility

Hard requirements and recommendation/background context without collapsing them into one prerequisite label.

exam_fees

Zero or more scoped fee observations. Each observation should retain its currency, audience or geography where relevant, source, and check date.

renewal

Lifecycle and maintenance information when attributable. Null remains valid when the public fact is unresolved.

preparation

Official and free preparation links and their evidence boundaries.

roles and occupations

Source-linked decision context, with occupation-level statistics kept distinct from credential-holder outcomes.

training_offers

Dated provider-owned observations, publicly restricted to the bounded three-credential set.

known_unknowns

Explicit unresolved public-fact fields. Their presence is part of the contract, not a data-quality score.

Schema-use rule: downstream code should tolerate nulls and unknown arrays. Do not coerce unresolved evidence to zero, false, “not offered,” or a recommendation.

Live URL projection

Twenty-eight exact matches; 22 deliberate nulls.

The v0.1 data release compared each normalized route_identifier with the 310-route RoleMath sitemap observed on 2026-08-10. Only exact matches received a rolemath_url.

  • No fuzzy matching.
  • No assumption that a successor credential inherited a retired route.
  • No projection from a held or private route.
  • No claim that sitemap membership equals indexing, impressions, or traffic.

Route distinction

route_identifier is the durable join key. rolemath_url is a dated live-route observation. Consumers should never substitute one for the other.

Private fields excluded

The public facts are not the private decision or review system.

The public export intentionally excludes the following classes of data and logic:

Excluded classExamplesWhy it is excluded
Registry and orderingtarget_rank, decision clusterA stable internal slot must not be misread as demand, recommendation, SEO, or implementation priority.
Recommendation logicDecision records, rankings, alternativesThe open fact layer should not silently expose or freeze private editorial logic.
Readiness and completenessReadiness profiles, completeness gates, freshness calculationsInternal release state is not a reader-facing quality percentage.
Review workflowConfidence, mapping confidence, source IDs, seed and review statusWorkflow metadata can be mistaken for factual certainty and may expose private operations.
Publication stateHeld, candidate, or private release statusA stable identifier must remain separate from a live public destination.
Commercial and personal dataPrivate provider research, matching logic, contact or inquiry dataNo private relationship, personal data, or commercial-routing logic belongs in the public release.

Known limitations

What the dataset does not prove.

  • Certification-caused salary, hiring, placement, promotion, pass rate, or return on investment.
  • Complete credential-market coverage or complete provider-market coverage.
  • Current training seats, future availability, provider willingness, fulfillment, authorization, or a commercial relationship.
  • That every record is complete merely because it is present.
  • That a non-null RoleMath URL is indexed, receiving impressions, or producing traffic.
  • That a null or unknown value is confirmed absent.
