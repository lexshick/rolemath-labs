Evidence contract

Currentness, scoped cost, eligibility, preparation, and context

Records separate hard prerequisites from recommended background, scope fees by currency and audience, retain lifecycle and renewal semantics, link official or free preparation, and carry checked dates plus explicit unresolved fields.

What it does not prove

Not certification-caused salary, hiring, placement, pass rate, ROI, current training seats, provider willingness, a provider relationship, traffic quality, leads, conversions, or revenue.
