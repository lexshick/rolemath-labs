What it contains

Currentness, cost, eligibility, preparation, and context

Records separate hard prerequisites from recommended background, scope fees by currency and audience, retain lifecycle and renewal semantics, link official/free preparation, and carry checked dates and known unknowns.

What it does not prove

Not certification-caused salary, hiring, placement, pass rate, ROI, training availability, provider willingness, a provider relationship, traffic, leads, conversions, or revenue.
