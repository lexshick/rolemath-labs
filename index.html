The product posture

Decide first. Source training later.

RoleMath separates credential fit, evidence, preparation, and observed training prices from provider sharing and commercial activation. The public sidecar contains no lead capture and makes no provider-relationship or availability claim.

Explicit limits

No certification-caused salary, hiring, pass rate, ROI, traffic, conversion, revenue, provider relationship, or employment outcome claim.
